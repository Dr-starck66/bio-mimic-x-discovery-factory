6. **US20210142173A1 — Network-based deep learning for target identification and drug repurposing**
   - Relevant overlap: AI/network target identification and drug repurposing.
   - Risk: generic machine-learning target discovery is crowded.

## BIO-MIMIC X differentiation worth preserving for counsel review

Potentially differentiating combinations, subject to professional prior-art analysis:

- explicit **claim-state machine** that separates VERIFIED / PLAUSIBLE / SPECULATIVE / UNSUPPORTED evidence;
- **negative knowledge memory** that preserves failed hypotheses and prevents silent re-promotion;
- **adversarial scientific falsification** before candidate promotion;
- cross-species translation that requires a provider-backed orthology/target chain and remains fail-closed when unresolved;
- **reproducible evidence snapshots with SHA-256** and replayable research state;
- explicit rule that animal evidence, orthology and target tractability cannot be promoted into human efficacy;
- integration of these gates into an autonomous research portfolio that can kill or pause programs rather than merely score them.

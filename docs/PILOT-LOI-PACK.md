# BIO-MIMIC X — Pilot / LOI Pack

## One-line positioning

BIO-MIMIC X is an evidence-first comparative-biology research engine that discovers cross-species mechanisms, attacks them adversarially, verifies translation paths and produces reproducible, falsifiable research dossiers.

## Pilot offer

**Pilot objective:** produce one research-ready, provenance-preserving biomimetic hypothesis for a partner-selected therapeutic or biological problem.

**Inputs from partner:** one problem statement, optional target area, and any internal exclusions.

**Outputs:**
- ranked cross-species candidate set;
- source-linked evidence ledger;
- negative evidence and rejection reasons;
- provider-backed orthology / human-target context where available;
- adversarial falsification report;
- one pre-registered falsification experiment;
- reproducible SHA-256 snapshot of the research state.

**What the pilot does not claim:** clinical efficacy, patentability, or replacement of wet-lab validation.

## Suggested pilot acceptance criteria

A pilot is successful only if:
1. at least one candidate survives all configured evidence and falsification gates;
2. all key claims have explicit provenance;
3. the final hypothesis includes a practical kill experiment;
4. unresolved translation or safety questions remain visible as PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED;
5. the partner scientist judges the dossier materially more useful than a standard literature search.

## Commercial proof ladder

- **Level 0 — UNVERIFIED:** no external partner.
- **Level 1 — VALIDATED INTEREST:** written external expert review supports further testing.
- **Level 2 — LOI:** partner signs a non-binding statement of intent to evaluate BIO-MIMIC X.
- **Level 3 — PAID PILOT:** paid research pilot or sponsored evaluation.
- **Level 4 — REPEAT USE:** second paid project or annual access agreement.
- **Level 5 — STRATEGIC VALUE:** proprietary validated candidate, licensing discussion or acquisition interest.

## Non-binding LOI template

**Parties:** BIO-MIMIC X and [Partner].

**Purpose:** evaluate BIO-MIMIC X for evidence-first comparative-biology hypothesis generation in [research area].

**Proposed evaluation:** one bounded pilot focused on [problem].

**Expected outputs:** evidence ledger, ranked candidates, negative-evidence record, translation checks, falsification plan and reproducible report.

**Confidentiality/IP:** each party retains pre-existing IP. New IP arising from the pilot will be handled under a separate written agreement before experimental work or disclosure beyond the evaluation team.

**Commercial intent:** if the pilot meets agreed acceptance criteria, the parties intend to discuss a paid continuation, license, research collaboration or software access arrangement.

**Non-binding:** this LOI records evaluation interest only and creates no obligation to purchase, license or fund work.

## Diligence rule

An unsigned template is not traction. BIO-MIMIC X may only upgrade the commercial gate after a signed LOI, paid pilot, or verifiable contract is archived with an immutable reference.

# BIO-MIMIC X Ω — Consolidated evidence audit of PR #45

**Review date:** 2026-11-12. **Audit reference:** [PR #45](https://github.com/Dr-starck66/bio-mimic-x-discovery-factory/pull/45), inspected at head `83bf4e6b5b3ccd83519c87250b1fd8e298eebfe7` (open/draft, unmerged). **Scope:** repository documents/JSON outputs and original-source comparison; this review did NOT repeat bulk RNA download or run the entire pipelines.

**Scientific conclusion:** The project documents real-data acquisition, identity/integrity gates, archived computations and negative findings. It has **not** demonstrated a statistically significant association for any of the three previously selected two-gene RNA proxies across their tested endpoints. Non-significance is not proof of zero effects. It has **not** demonstrated a clinical biomarker, a cretostimogene-specific predictor, a therapeutic benefit, or a commercial agreement.

## Claims audit

**Demonstrated in repository artifacts:** real public-data provenance; 35, 40 and 94 sample analyses; strict identity/fingerprint controls; audit trails; targeted self-tests and documented separate cross-language recomputation for GSE244895; negative evidence after multiplicity adjustment; retained malformed-row exception and HfRI deviations. This review independently checked the published NCBI series descriptions and the reported BH adjustments.

**Not demonstrated:** statistically significant performance for the three selected immune proxies; stable biological mechanism; equivalence of the HfRI replay with authors' R implementation; independent prospective clinical validation; cretostimogene-treated patient data or prediction of their outcomes; improved response, patient survival or quality of life; wet-lab efficacy; regulatory clearance; connection with or approval by CG Oncology; customer agreement, revenue, or company valuation.

**Distinctions:** negative exploratory results ≠ proof no biological effect; a computational checksum/test PASS ≠ clinical validation; GSE294705 recurrence ≠ GSE176178 BCG durable response ≠ GSE244895 matched pre/post. None of these datasets can establish cretostimogene-specific therapeutic improvement.

## Scientifically defensible text (FR)

> BIO-MIMIC X a réalisé une réanalyse exploratoire et traçable de trois séries transcriptomiques publiques relatives au cancer de la vessie non infiltrant le muscle : GSE244895 (comparaisons avant/après BCG sur 12 patients appariés), GSE176178 (40 tumeurs avec statut de réponse durable au BCG) et GSE294705 (94 patients avec critère d'au moins deux récidives). Les contrôles documentés d'intégrité et d'identité des échantillons, ainsi que les analyses statistiques archivées, permettent une vérification technique. Les critères de jugement diffèrent entre cohortes et ne constituent pas une réplication clinique directe. Une implémentation approximative du score HfRI publié a également été calculée sur GSE294705, sans reproduction fidèle encore démontrée de son calculateur original. Aucune validation clinique prospective, aucun prédicteur de réponse au cretostimogene et aucun bénéfice thérapeutique ne sont établis.

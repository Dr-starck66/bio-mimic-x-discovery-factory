## Ranking logic

The engine prioritises what BIO-MIMIC X should test first using:

- reported removal performance;
- reuse-cycle evidence;
- retained performance after reuse;
- real-water/wastewater evidence;
- magnetic recoverability;
- explicit risk penalties for problematic material features.

The score is **not** a safety score or an efficacy certification.

## Falsification before promotion

Every architecture must survive the same comparative program:

- identical polymer loading versus the Ti3C2Tx@Ni baseline;
- quantitative magnetic recovery mass balance;
- particle/metal/coating release assay;
- PS + PET + PE challenge;
- natural-water or wastewater challenge;
- ten-cycle durability;
- matched ecotoxicity controls.

No candidate can be marked safe or field-ready automatically.

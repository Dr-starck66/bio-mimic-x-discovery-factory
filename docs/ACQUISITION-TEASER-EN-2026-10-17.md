# BIO-MIMIC X — Confidentiality-Safe Technology Acquisition Overview
**Prepared 17 October 2026 — non-confidential first-contact edition**

## The opportunity
BIO-MIMIC X is an operating, source-linked comparative-biology research workflow for identifying biological hypotheses, preserving contradictory and negative evidence, assessing translational feasibility, and recording reproducible analysis decisions. It is a **research software asset**, not an experimentally validated therapy, novel drug program, or regulated medical device.

**Transaction under discussion:** potential full transfer of the legally transferable codebase and associated know-how, subject to evidence of ownership, open-source compliance, institutional rights, export controls (if any), contractual scope and buyer diligence.

**Indicative seller asking price:** **EUR 1,000,000**, negotiable in a formal transaction process. This is neither an independent valuation nor a buyer offer; no deal is signed or agreed.

## Why potentially useful to a pharma-AI buyer
- Differentiable science-workflow orchestration: source → species → phenotype → mechanistic hypothesis → competing evidence → kill experiment.
- Real API connections in browser to Europe PMC; Ensembl and Open Targets paths implemented, requiring endpoint-by-endpoint audit.
- Persistent negative knowledge, evidence-status controls and SHA-256 provenance / replay.
- Research portfolio triage, adversarial hypothesis review and explicit fail-closed logic against unjustified biological claims.
- Use as a complementary hypothesis-audit layer, not a substitute for laboratory discovery or proprietary multimodal data.

## A 20-minute buyer screening
1. Product walkthrough: provenance ledger, review/refutation, negative memory, replay checksum.
2. Buyer fit: one team-specific task in target/fibrosis research and acceptance criteria.
3. Scope: source and artifacts being transferred, contributions, licenses, data rights, hosting and documentation.
4. Deal structure: clean asset transfer, optional technical handover, reasonable due-diligence and escrow/closing conditions.
5. If mutual interest: NDA and access-controlled technical data room with code provenance and IP schedules.

## Important disclosure rule
Do not claim exclusive patented biology, patent freedom-to-operate, therapy efficacy, an observed new mechanism, commercial revenue, binding offers or guaranteed prices. This public overview must not include customer data, unpublished trade secrets or personal access credentials. A public repository can be read by prospective buyers and competitors: counsel must assess the effects of prior public disclosure on novelty and deal exclusivity.

# BIO-MIMIC X — RNA Evidence Lab: independent research pilot
Date: 2026-11-18

## Audience and scope
Research groups and biotech teams working on oocyte development, organoid phenotyping, transcriptional regulation, or RNA-seq data integrity.
This is a proposed commercial research-software service, not an accepted sales contract, regulated diagnostic, clinical device, or licence to the Zollo body discovery.

## Available demonstrator
https://bio-mimic-x-industrial-evidence-if9m9p.v2.appdeploy.ai/rna-evidence/

Current functions: browser-local import of six-column integer count data, DESeq-style median-ratio library-size normalization, exploratory 3-vs-3 effect ranking, exhaustive 20 split global negative control, CSV/JSON export. Deterministic synthetic data only in the published example. No real, private, or patient data are transmitted by the software.
This is an exploratory audit. No DESeq2 generalized-linear negative-binomial differential test, gene-level BH FDR, wet-lab validation, clinical performance, or therapeutic effect is claimed.

## Proposed paid pilot: indicative offer, not a price validated with a buyer
Fee target: EUR 12,500 excl. VAT, subject to agreed project scope, data rights and procurement.
Indicative duration: 3–4 weeks after signing and lawful delivery of an adequately licensed partner-selected dataset.
Inputs: partner-selected non-patient or legally authorized de-identified RNA-seq count matrix; study design; independent reference pipeline.
Deliverables: provenance ledger, quality-control report, selected effect-size candidates, negative-control comparisons, source reproducibility package, and technical meeting.
Acceptance: runnable script plus inputs known to the customer, reproducible outputs within preagreed tolerances, record of failed tests, and documented comparison to a conventional statistical workflow.
Out of scope: diagnostics, IVF intervention, new biological proof, clinical validation, genome-wide uniqueness claims, publication of third-party data without consent.

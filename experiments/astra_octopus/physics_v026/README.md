# ASTRA PHYSICS ADAPTIVE Ω v0.26
Exploratory physics-informed pump leakage diagnosis. No new blind physical validation or deployment.
## Data and method
Reuses v0.25 parser and audited cycle-level UCI hydraulic means, pinned blob ef3eaa673310ffa2f2693d2b74d7c757f388037e, 2205 cycles, 10 selected sensors. Reference UCI DOI 10.24432/C5CW21. Raw waveforms not retrieved. Physics-guided features: log of proxy (5/3)*PS1_mean*FS1_mean/EPS1_mean (bar and L/min to W); log pressure ratios PS2/PS1 and PS3/PS1, flow ratio FS1/FS2 and specific motor power EPS1/PS1. A true hydraulic power uses pressure differential and simultaneous flow; mean product differs from mean instantaneous product and measurement locations/shaft losses matter. This proxy is not a certified energy balance or measured pump efficiency. SE is a supplied virtual efficiency channel, used as separate comparator and in fallback, not in learned features. No claim of independent witnesses.
Compare fixed balanced logistic fit on 9 raw means excluding SE versus 5 derived features versus both, with direct proxy, SE and flow thresholds. Same fit/regularization for learned models. Supervised training uses pump labels; not directly equivalent to one-class CUSUM. Thresholds on calibration only: nominal 95th normal score quantile (strict exceedance), or 5th percentile of each leakage severity score (inclusive, minimum of both). No test label in score. Whole cooler-condition groups held out in three cyclic train/cal/test assignments; distinct test rows across these 3 folds; same rig, not independent trials. Cooler degradation differs from normal load variation; do not generalize to arbitrary operating conditions. All data previously exposed in v0.25. Previous chronological split repeats prior test, not additional independent evidence.
## Results
Previous chronological 735 test cycles: at calibration recall95 threshold raw logistic 70 TP/0 FP; combined 328 TP/0 FP; proxy and supplied SE also 328 TP/0 FP; flow 195 TP/0 FP. At fpr5 calibration raw 169 TP/0 FP, combined 328 TP/5 FP, flow 328 TP/0 FP. Across 3 cooler holdouts, total 984 leakage and 1221 no-leak cycles: fpr5 combined 972 TP/19 FP, raw 777 TP/19 FP; recall95 combined 913 TP/15 FP, raw 468 TP/15 FP. Direct indicators sometimes match or exceed combined; see aggregate.json. No >=50% FPR reduction over same supervised baseline at same sensitivity demonstrated across holdouts. Prior CUSUM failed on this partition (406 FP) but different supervision and exposed test preclude claiming superiority. Exact replay of core result.
## Missingness
Independent per-channel random masking of already aggregated test features, not physical sensor outage; 10 seeds each at 10% and 25%. First fallback (full, physical logistic, proxy, flow, SE) increases coverage but introduces FP. Post-audit revised priority full, proxy, supplied SE, flow, tested with 10 new mask seeds per rate. Thresholds unchanged, same previously exposed 735 test cycles. At 10% masked: mean 99.0068% coverage, 98.4756% recall, 0 FP; at 25%: 93.7143% coverage, 89.9695% recall, 0 FP. Unknown faults count as missed. Masked SE treated independently of underlying sensors, optimistic since SE may depend on them. Real correlated outages, physical bias and dynamic regime changes not tested. No imputation. First fallback retained with negative results. Unit conversion, missing pressure, zero power, label independence and deterministic replay checks pass in initial mask test.

# ASTRA DUAL INVARIANT Ω v0.23
Exploratory dual-residual monitoring. Not a physical first-integral proof, safety system, or RUL estimator.
Parent: predictive_v022, robust median/MAD normalization, causal 5-sample median, 3-sample persistence, first 100 observations for fitting, next 100 for calibration, 99th empirical quantile. Absolute = max absolute standardized deviation; relative = max deviation from median standardized channel. Dual = maximum of both scores divided by their respective 95th calibration quantiles; single joint 99th percentile threshold. References: each component, RMS of standardized signals, EWMA of max absolute score, absolute median of standardized signals. Reset persistence on gaps >3 median initial sampling intervals or insufficient observed channels. Minimum 75% observed channels and at least 3. No trained physical law or causal attribution. Same nominal calibration quantile does not equal same actual FPR or same probability of an alarm over a whole trajectory.
